# ECHOES — Narrative

Main arc, endings, side threads, and the dialogue style guide with samples. Character depth lives in [04-characters](04-characters.md).

---

## 1. Main arc — "The Second Ringing"

### Act I — The Waterline (Fathom → Tolls)

You wake face-down at the Hush's floodline with a cracked bell-shard fused in your sternum and no name — not forgotten: *unwritten*. The drowned parishes half-know you; the Choir calls you a sign; the Escapement's bulletins call you a misfiled soul to be returned for filing.

**Beats:** Bellkeeper Ambrose finds you at the Vestry (hub) and teaches the shard's use — Tier I slips at fissures. The Bailiff falls. In the Tolls you learn what the city is: rationed hours, viewing-hour parlors, the Hush eating the lower floors while Clockrow stamps forms insisting otherwise. First Vesper encounter — she doesn't fight you; she *reads* you, and leaves shaken by what isn't on the page.

**Act question planted:** everyone who looks at you too long becomes briefly, terribly sad. Ambrose most of all.

### Act II — The Climb (Weld → Clockrow)

**Beats:** The Stitchguild takes you on as the ultimate slip-runner. Mara maps your fissures; her price is the Residency thread's opening (below). Cast-Mother Sefa falls and the Long Forge lights — the Guild owes you; the Order notices you. Tier II.

Clockrow: the Guild's real job for you — crack the rebuilt **Census of Hours** and find who you were. Your record exists. One line: ***"UNWRITTEN, year 0. By the Grace of the Order. See Ossuary."*** Vesper duel #1 in the burning stacks — she has now read the same line, and zealotry with a paper cut bleeds doubt.

**Midpoint revelation (the Ossuary ledger fragment, corroborated by thread 3):** the Null Chime was rung by a lowborn hand — the Bellkeeper's own apprentice, coerced with the lives of the Tolls as hostage, then erased. Ambrose was the master who gave up his apprentice. You are what the apprentice became. You didn't survive the Fracture. You *are* it.

### Act III — The Lantern

**Beats:** Passage to the Lantern needs a clean hour — obtained through faction commitment (Guild's Great Slip, the Assembly's Residency corruption, or the Choir's quiet doors; each route burns a bridge). The Ossuary of Unstruck Hours: everything the Order erased, shelved and labeled, including the Vigil of the Ford, including a small parish coat with your shard-shaped burn in it. Vesper resolution (ally, corpse, or Custodian — thread 2 decides which). Then the Anchorite's Cell.

The Anchorite — the first Bellkeeper, wound into the Meridian's chains for sixty-one years, holding the three shards apart with what remains of a self — speaks in three tenses at once and offers no defense of the Order. Only the situation: *the chime is still ringing. It has been ringing for sixty-one years. Someone must decide what it was for.*

You stand where you stood. The hammer is still warm. Three choices.

### The three endings

| Ending | Act | Cost | Final image |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Let It Strike** | Complete the Null Chime. The Eve becomes the only truth; the Ascension never happens; the Order's golden city persists, whole and clean. | Everyone in the Now — everyone you met — is unwritten. Mara, mid-sentence. | Market-day forever. The same bread to the same hands. Somewhere in the crowd, a bellkeeper's apprentice who will never be asked. |
| **Still the Bell** | Break the Meridian's chains and silence the Great Carillon. The shards collapse into the Now; the Hush stops; *clean time ends* — no more tithes, no more banked centuries, no more Order. | The Eve (and every looping figure in it) is gone; the city must age honestly, together, starting now. Hard dawn. | Terrace-folk grey in a season. In the Tolls, for the first time in sixty-one years, a day happens *once*. |
| **The Anchor** | Take the Anchorite's place. The shards hold; the Hush recedes to the waterline; the city continues — divided, unjust, alive — with you as its hidden keel. | You. Forever. And the machine you now maintain is the machine that erased you. | Ambrose climbs down to the Vestry each Vespers and rings once, for someone he is no longer able to name. He no longer knows why. He never misses it. |

No ending is graded. Item lore and NPC epilogue lines carry the consequences; there is no narrator.

## 2. Side threads

### Thread 1 — The Residency *(Mara · Orven · the Assembly)*

The Order secretly sells the wealthy **residencies in the Eve** — one-way emigration into the kept day, body and all, installed into the loop like furniture. Mara's family has been "resident" since she was nine; she pays for viewing-hours to watch them loop. Magistrate Orven, three centuries old and coming apart, wants to buy his way in entirely.

**Arc:** authenticate the ledger → witness an installation (the game's quietest horror scene: a smiling family fitted into a day that isn't theirs) → choose: **expose it** (Now riots; Escapement crackdown; Terraces gate opens by force), **enable Orven's passage** (his estate and centuries of banked hours become yours; his epilogue line in the Eve is the worst sentence in the game), or **burn the ledger** (no one gets in again — including Mara's last hope of joining her family). Feeds the Act III clean-hour gate. **Theme: nostalgia as real estate.**

### Thread 2 — The Quiet Doors *(Vesper · the Choir)*

The Choir is opening "quiet doors" in the Tolls — hastening the Hush into inhabited floors, gently, with hymns, calling it mercy. Their prophet, the **Threnodist**, preaches from inside the fog and is never wrong about what the Order will do next — because she is a future echo of Sister Vesper, leaked backward through the Fathom's fissures.

**Arc:** trace the doors → meet the Threnodist and *recognize her* → choose whether Now-Vesper learns what she becomes. Show her: she shatters — and reassembles as an ally against the Lantern (skipping her Custodian fight, gaining the game's best co-op boss ally). Conceal it: she meets her echo unprepared at the worst moment and the Vesper Custodian fight becomes unavoidable — and worse, because now she's fighting *that*, through you. **Theme: whether foreknowledge is a gift or a sentence.**

### Thread 3 — The Vigil of the Ford *(Hollow-of-Bells)*

A Remnant knight in ford-grey armor guards a bridge that isn't there, sworn to an order that never existed. Only you perceive him whole. His quest: **proof** — any single object that remembers the Vigil.

**Arc:** four relic-traces across all three states (a whetstone in the Eve that sharpens nothing; a Hush-preserved standard; the Ossuary's sealed Vigil shelf) → the revelation that arms the endgame: **the Null Chime was not the first**. The Order has erased before — the Vigil was unmade in the Concordance for objecting to the machine at its birth. The lie is not sixty-one years old; it is the foundation. → At thread's end he asks you to hold the proof *and let him fade witnessed*, or to drive it into his breastplate — anchoring him: real, permanent, and alone in a world with no Vigil in it. **Theme: whether being remembered is the same as having existed.**

## 3. Dialogue style guide

### The rules

1. **NPCs answer their own preoccupations, not the player's quest log.** Ask Ambrose about the Order; he tells you about a bell he failed to maintain in 34. The information is *in* there.
2. **The world answers first.** If a Glimpse can show it, no one says it. Dialogue confirms and colors; it never tours.
3. **Repetition as liturgy.** Exhausted dialogue loops to a short *coda line* — and the coda is where the character lives. Codas change with story state; players learn to re-visit after every major beat.
4. **No question marks from NPCs to the player** (almost). The player is the asked-about, never the interviewer's subject. Rhetorical exceptions earn their weight.
5. **Sentence music:** short declaratives; archaic rhythm, not archaic vocabulary — no "thee," no "'tis." Concrete nouns (bells, papers, bread, water) over abstractions. If a line explains, cut it; if it *aches* and happens to inform, keep it.
6. **Laughter, pauses, and trailing off are written into the line**, soulslike fashion: "Hah… ha." does more work than a paragraph.
7. **State-variant lines are the signature move.** The same NPC met across shards continues, contradicts, or pre-empts themself. Budget: key NPCs get per-state pools; minor NPCs get one state only.

### Samples (canon until improved)

**Ambrose — first meeting (Now, the Vestry):**
> "Still. Be still a moment. Let me hear what you are."
> *(a long pause; he does not face you)*
> "…Misfiled. Hm. The clock takes; the clock forgets to take. It happens. It happened.
> There is a bell above the water that still wants ringing. Start there. Climbing has always been this city's answer, for whatever that is worth. It has never been much."

**Ambrose — coda, early:** "Mind the water. It remembers being higher."
**Ambrose — coda, after the Census revelation:** "…You've been reading. Hm. Old men keep what they cannot repair. Ask, or do not."

**Vesper — first encounter (Tolls, no combat):**
> "Papers.
> *(beat)*
> No. No, you wouldn't have papers, would you. There's no *line* for you at all.
> …I am going to have to write a great many reports about you. Go. Before I decide the reports are worth it."

**Mara — teaching Tier II (Weld):**
> "Rules first. You slip where I've mapped, you pay what you owe, you don't talk to my crew about my family. That's all of them.
> Fissures are the city being honest for once. Everywhere else, it lies at you. So: everywhere else is where the money is."

**The Threnodist — from inside the fog:**
> "You climb. They all climb. Sixty-one years of ladders.
> The quiet is not coming for you, bell-child. It is *waiting* for you. These are different kindnesses."

**Hollow-of-Bells — bridge that isn't there:**
> "Hold, traveler. The ford is watched.
> …You see the ford. *(a sound like a laugh from inside a helm)* You see it. Ah. Then stand a moment with me, and let two things that are not here keep each other company."

**Orven — the Twice-Gilded:**
> "Three hundred years, and I can tell you the price of every one. To the minute. To the *chit*.
> I have my daughters' accounts in a drawer somewhere. Their faces, no. But the accounts are very complete."

**The Anchorite — the Cell:**
> "I held. I am holding. I will have held.
> You came back — no. Forgive me. The tenses. *You are the one who comes back.*
> The hammer is where you are leaving it. Decide what the ringing was for. No one else living was there."

### Item-lore samples

> **Cordial of Kept Hours** — *Distilled from tithed time; the Order's mercy, sold by the mouthful. Drink, and be briefly the person you were before the wound.*

> **Hour-chit (docked)** — *A Weld laborer's wage, stamped VOID — REPEATED DAY. The Tolls have a saying: work it twice, keep it never.*

> **Ford-grey whetstone** — *It sharpens nothing. It is certain it used to.*

> **Escapement writ of return** — *"The bearer is to be filed correctly." Nothing on the writ says where, or as what.*

> **Bell-shard (your sternum)** — *A piece of something that broke sixty-one years ago and has not finished breaking. It is warm at Vespers. It points up.*
